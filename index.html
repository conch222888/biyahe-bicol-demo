Help & Travel Info

Travel advisories

  • Road works along Andaya Highway (Quezon) may add 30 to 45 minutes to night trips this week.
  • During typhoon signals, trips can be held at the terminal. Check Live Status before leaving home.
  • Peak dates (Undas, Christmas, Holy Week) sell out early. Book at least a week ahead.

Terminals

Baggage

  • Free: one checked bag up to 20 kg in the hold, plus one small hand-carry on board.
  • Excess baggage: ₱20 per kg, paid at the counter. Single pieces over 30 kg travel as cargo.
  • Tag every bag with your name and mobile number. Keep cash, gadgets and medicine with you.
  • Not allowed: flammables, firearms, strong-smelling items such as durian, and unpacked fresh fish.

Pets

  • Small cats and dogs (up to 10 kg) are welcome on Standard coaches, in a closed, leak-proof carrier.
  • One pet per passenger, ₱150 per trip. The carrier stays at your feet, not on a seat.
  • Bring the vaccination card. The pet should wear a diaper for the whole trip.
  • Pets are not carried in First Class cabins or in the baggage hold.

Terms and privacy

Sample wording for this demo. Have your counsel and your Data Protection Officer review and replace it before launch.

Terms of service and fare rules

  1. Your ticket. A paid booking is a contract of carriage for the named passengers, date, bus and seats. The QR code is the ticket. One scan boards the party; a copy that has already been scanned is refused.
  2. Fares. Fares are per passenger and exclude the terminal fee. Friday and Sunday fares are higher and Tuesday and Wednesday fares are lower than the regular fare. The fare you pay is the one shown at checkout.
  3. Discounts. Senior citizens, persons with disability, students and children 3 to 11 pay 20% less on presenting the matching valid ID at boarding. Without the ID, the fare difference is collected.
  4. Changes. You may rebook up to 4 hours before departure for ₱100 per passenger. If your seats are taken on the new date you are given the nearest free ones.
  5. Cancellations and refunds. 80% is refunded when you cancel 24 hours or more before departure, 50% between 24 and 4 hours, and nothing after that. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 3 to 5 banking days.
  6. Trips we cancel. If we cancel a trip, you choose a free rebooking to any date within 30 days or a full refund.
  7. Boarding. Be at the terminal 30 minutes before departure. Boarding closes 10 minutes before the bus leaves and a no-show ticket is forfeited.
  8. Baggage, pets and cargo. The limits and fees on the Help page form part of these terms. We may refuse items that are unsafe or prohibited by law.
  9. Liability. Our liability for delay, baggage and injury is as provided by Philippine law on common carriers. Optional travel insurance is provided by the named insurer under its own policy.

Privacy notice (Republic Act 10173)

What we collect. Passenger names, fare type, mobile number, optional email, booking and payment references, seat and trip details, and, if you sign in, your saved passengers and points. We do not store card numbers or wallet PINs; payments are handled by the payment provider.

Why we use it. To issue and check your ticket, send booking messages, handle changes and refunds, meet legal and safety duties such as passenger manifests, and, only if you opt in, send promos.

Who sees it. Our terminal, gate and dispatch staff for the trips you book, our payment and SMS providers, and government agencies when the law requires. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it. Booking records are kept for as long as tax and transport rules require, then deleted or anonymised. You can delete a saved passenger or your account at any time.

Your rights. You may ask to see, correct, or delete your data, object to marketing, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Write to our Data Protection Officer through the contact details on the Help page.

FOR BUS OPERATORS

Sell every seat online, and run the terminal from one screen.

One system for the passenger app, the counter, the gate and dispatch, all reading the same seat map. Everything on this site is a working demo of it.

What your passengers get

Seat-level booking

Either direction, one way or round trip, up to ten passengers, with a seat chosen for each one.

Local payments

GCash, Maya, QR Ph and cards, with seats held while they pay and a clean retry when a payment fails.

A ticket that works offline

A signed QR code saved on the phone. Rebooking, refunds, reminders and delay alerts without a phone call.

English and Filipino

The whole passenger side switches language, and installs to the home screen like an app.

Live bus status

A route map and arrival board, so passengers and the people meeting them stop calling the terminal.

Cargo and add-ons

Parcel drop-offs, extra baggage, pets and insurance sold in the same checkout.

What your team gets

Dispatch

Every departure with seats sold and revenue, buses and drivers assigned, delays pushed to passengers in one tap, fares, promo codes and advisories changed without a developer.

Counter

Walk-in sales on the same seat map as online sales, so a seat is never sold twice. The manifest prints from the same list.

Gate

Scan to board. Wrong date, already boarded, cancelled and tampered tickets are each caught and explained.

Driver

Assigned trips, the head count against scanned tickets, and trip start and arrival sent to dispatch.
